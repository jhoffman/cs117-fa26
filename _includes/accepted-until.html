{%- comment -%} Last day an assignment is accepted: its `late_until` date if set, "none" if late_until is "none", otherwise the due date plus the course-wide grace period (site.grace_days). Adding grace_days plus half a day and keeping only the date avoids an off-by-one when a daylight-saving change falls in the window. Sets `accepted_until` (YYYY-MM-DD or "none") for the caller. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.item.late_until == "none" -%} {%- assign accepted_until = "none" -%} {%- elsif include.item.late_until -%} {%- assign accepted_until = include.item.late_until | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign grace_seconds = site.grace_days | times: 86400 | plus: 43200 -%} {%- assign accepted_until = include.item.due | date: "%s" | plus: grace_seconds | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- endif -%}

## Academic Integrity

Academic dishonesty will not be tolerated. This includes cheating, plagiarism, and helping others commit a violation. You are expected to know and follow the [UCI Academic Integrity Policy](https://aisc.uci.edu/).

You should not view or copy anyone else's code, and you should not post solution code on Ed Discussion. You may build on third-party code or datasets in the final project as long as you clearly cite them and make clear what is your own work.
